Creative Director Pt. 1 Summer of Love 1969 Brand Style Guide

What happens when the golden haze of California flower power breaks, turns feral, and hits the wet, cold pavement of Manhattan at midnight?

Summer of Love 1969 is Free People’s nocturnal subversion of commercial bohemianism. Moving past sun-washed fields and festival clichés, this campaign captures the intoxicating tension between California desert mysticism and the raw, romantic danger of late-1960s New York.

The collection lives in the friction between two icons: the sweeping, ritualistic silk chiffon of Stevie Nicks and the razor-sharp, androgynous tailoring of Patti Smith inside the Chelsea Hotel. Floor-grazing crushed oxblood velvet dusters drape over shredded muslin slips; battered cowhide fringe meets low-rise raw denim; hand-hammered Navajo silver and turquoise amulets catch the glare of streetlamps and oncoming headlights. Designed for asphalt rather than pastures, Summer of Love 1969 is dark romance with a razor’s edge.

01 // Visual Identity

Logomark & Typographic Seals

The Third-Eye Linocut Seal

Spiritual campaign emblem. Sourced from 1970s psychedelic concert prints; represents the occult mysticism of the desert commune. Applied to metal hardware, coin pendants, and packaging seals.

Desert Heritage Origin Badge

The narrative origin insignia. Featuring heavy 1970s psychedelic bubble typography, California poppies, and the sun rising over desert bluffs. Used on embroidered jacket linings, denim leather back-patches, and hangtags.

Guerrilla Street Stamp

The raw Manhattan endorsement mark. Scaled for wheatpasting onto East Village brick walls, subway corridor posters, and stamped lookbook covers for the NYC campaign launch.

03 // Typography Hierarchy

Brand Campaign Fonts

Primary Campaign Headline & Wordmark
Rhiannon Rings Like a Bell Through the Night

Playfair Display / Swash Roman: Sourced from late-1970s folk-rock album sleeves (Fleetwood Mac’s Rumours). High-contrast stems, dramatic italic postures, and sweeping teardrop terminals for luxury fashion headlines.

Occult Emblem & Sacred Linework
Know Your Power · August 1969

UnifrakturCook / Antique Gothic: Reserved for sacred seals, linocut woodblocks, occult graphic inscriptions, and garment embroidery badges.

Technical Metadata & Dispatch Transcripts
DISPATCH: ROOM 1017 CHELSEA HOTEL // 04:00 AM // ALL ASSETS ON ASPHALT

Space Mono: Emulates the mechanical typewriter output of 1969 police reports, dispatch slips, and hotel ledgers. Used for editorial captions, timestamps, and ad specs.
