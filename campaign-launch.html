Campaign Manifesto • Fall 1969

Dark Romance With a Razor’s Edge

“When the golden haze of California flower power breaks, turns feral, and hits the wet midnight pavement of Manhattan.”

Summer of Love 1969 is Free People’s nocturnal subversion of commercial bohemianism. Moving beyond sun-washed festival clichés, the collection lives in the electric friction between two icons: the sweeping, ritualistic silk chiffon of Stevie Nicks and the razor-sharp, androgynous tailoring of Patti Smith inside the Chelsea Hotel.

Floor-grazing crushed oxblood velvet dusters drape over shredded muslin slips; battered cowhide fringe meets low-rise raw denim; hand-hammered Navajo silver and turquoise amulets catch the glare of oncoming headlights along Route 66.

Key Silhouettes Shearling Tapestry Coats, Flared Corduroy, Muslin Slips
Hardware & Trim Bone Chokers, Turquoise Inlays, Antique Brass
Staging Canvas Route 66 Motels, W 4th St Platform, Chelsea Hotel
